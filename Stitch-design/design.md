## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an uncompromising, technical consultancy vernacular for an independent engineering practice. Rejecting generic corporate SaaS abstractions and neon "cyberpunk" tropes, the aesthetic is rooted in surgical precision, editorial rigor, and computational clarity.

The visual identity embodies the spirit of an elite technical problem-solver: thoughtful, authoritative, and direct. The visual hierarchy relies on crisp architectural grid alignments, generous spatial breathing room, and high typographic contrast. The tension between razor-sharp geometric headlines and utilitarian body typography communicates mastery over complex software architectures and digital craftsmanship. The interface feels less like a templated dashboard and more like a high-end technical whitepaper or an engineer's bespoke command bench.

## Colors

The system employs a deeply disciplined chromatic rhythm built on deep blue-black foundations, rich slate transitions, and deliberate visual accents.

### Foundations & Surfaces

- **Canvas (`#0B0D12`)**: The foundational canvas pitch. It holds subtle cool-blue undertones, avoiding flat, dead neutrals.
- **Surface Tiers (`#10131A`, `#161A23`, `#1E2330`)**: Surfaces rise through controlled luminance shifts, preserving a cool, technical atmosphere without muddying typographic contrast.
- **Structural Borders (`#262D3D`, `#333C52`)**: Deliberate hairline strokes establish structural hierarchy. Borders define visual boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Editorial & Text Tones

- **Primary Text (`#F4F4F0`)**: A warm off-white that softens terminal glare while retaining high contrast against dark foundations.
- **Secondary Text (`#A6ABB8`)**: A muted blue-gray for supporting copy, narrative prose, and metadata.
- **Muted Text (`#687182`)**: A low-contrast slate reserved strictly for non-critical timestamps, indices, and inactive states.

### Chromatic Hierarchy & Allocation

- **Brand Red (`#FF3B30` / `#E62E24`)**: Confined to an 8–12% global coverage ceiling. It serves strictly as an operational accent: primary calls to action, active navigation coordinates, sequential numbering (`01`, `02`), terminal prompts, and critical word-level focal points. It is never applied to broad background containers.
- **Steel-Blue Metadata (`#5C7C99`, `#4B6B88`, `#8FAFC8`)**: Identifies technical infrastructure, architectural labels, code scopes, and system telemetry.
- **Warm Amber (`#D48B38`, `#E5A454`)**: Deployed sparingly to provide human warmth, contextual warnings, or secondary highlighting.
- **Semantic Badges**: Restrained emerald green (`#34C759`) denotes live/shipped states, amber (`#D48B38`) indicates in-progress systems, and steel-blue (`#5C7C99`) represents informative notices.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a sharp dialogue between structural geometric display elements and legible human prose.

- **Headlines & Structure (`Geist`)**: Chosen for its surgical precision and tight kerning. Large scale display headers employ heavy weight and tight tracking to anchor the page, echoing the mechanical clarity of modern engineering artifacts.
- **Narrative Prose (`Source Sans 3`)**: Balances the severe geometry of the headlines with human warmth and readability. The generous line heights (1.55 to 1.65) ensure sustained comfort across technical essays, project narratives, and technical explanations.
- **Indexing & Accents**: Sequence markers (such as `01`, `02`) utilize high-contrast geometric numerals set in primary brand red. Labels, tech tags, and infrastructure notation leverage medium-weight uppercase letterforms with extended letter-spacing to reinforce technical rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is defined by asymmetric grid discipline and expansive negative space. Content relies on horizontal datum rules and whitespace to separate ideas rather than stacking repetitive container cards.

### Grid Architecture

- **Desktop (1280px Max Content Width)**: A 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Sections favor uneven splits (such as 4:8 for index-to-content or 5:7 for narrative-to-telemetry) to avoid standard SaaS dashboard patterns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: Transitions to an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` side padding.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: Collapses into a 4-column single-axis flow with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas margins. Full-bleed structural hairline dividers ground the mobile vertical rhythm.

### Spacing Principles

All spacing adheres strictly to a 4px/8px modular base. Content sections use generous vertical spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) anchored by continuous 1px rules across the page width.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural tonal surfaces and hairline boundaries, deliberately avoiding diffuse drop shadows and gratuitous glassmorphic blurs.

### Tonal Hierarchy

- **Level 0 (Canvas `#0B0D12`)**: The deep foundation upon which layout lines and editorial columns are drawn.
- **Level 1 (Structural Panels `#10131A`)**: Baseline component fills, section backgrounds, and technical matrices.
- **Level 2 (Active Modules `#161A23`)**: Interactive list highlights, raised terminal blocks, and focused work viewports.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs `#1E2330`)**: Popovers, command palettes, and transient navigation menus.

### Structural Hairlines

Depth transitions are punctuated by 1px precision strokes:

- Standard separators: `#262D3D`
- Active, hover, or focused boundaries: `#333C52` or the primary brand red (`#FF3B30`).
  Diffused drop shadows are replaced by high-contrast tonal containment, creating a clean, schematic presentation.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, subtle shape language (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corners sharp and deliberate.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to interactive elements including buttons, inputs, filter chips, and code snippets.
- **Container Radius (0.375rem to 0.5rem / 6px to 8px)**: Reserved for project media previews and major viewport frames.
- **Strict Hairline Strokes**: Component perimeters maintain a precise 1px stroke.
- **Geometric Restraint**: Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly forbidden for buttons and panels to prevent visual softening. Sharp terminal corners (0px) are intentionally used on full-width layout bands and table header boundaries to reinforce technical discipline.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action**: Solid `#FF3B30` background with dark foundation text (`#0B0D12`). Styled using `Geist` uppercase (`label-md`). 4px border radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Accompanied by a trailing directional arrow (`→`) spaced by 8px. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#E62E24`.
- **Secondary / Technical**: Transparent fill, 1px border `#262D3D`, text `#F4F4F0`. Hover state transitions the border to `#333C52` with a subtle surface lift (`#161A23`).
- **Text Link Action**: Borderless, `#F4F4F0` text, highlighting to `#FF3B30` on hover with a 1px baseline underline offset by 4px.

### Metadata Chips & Status Badges

- **Technical Tag**: Monospaced look via small `Geist` uppercase tracking (`label-sm`). Background `#10131A`, 1px border `#262D3D`, text `#8FAFC8`.
- **Status Indicator**: Compact badge housing a 6px solid circular node. Shipped status uses a `#34C759` indicator on a `#161A23` pill-free container; In-progress items employ `#D48B38`.

### Form Fields & Inputs

- **Input Field**: Background `#10131A`, 1px border `#262D3D`, text `#F4F4F0`, font `Source Sans 3` (`body-md`). Radius 4px. Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Active focus shifts the border stroke to a crisp `#FF3B30` with zero blur halo. Placeholder text is rendered in `#687182`.

### Checkboxes & Radios

- **Selection**: 16px × 16px footprint with a 2px radius for checkboxes and circular geometry for radios. Inactive state: `#10131A` fill with a `#333C52` border. Checked state: `#FF3B30` fill and border containing a clean, sharp 1.5px monochrome check glyph.

### Editorial Lists & Project Rows

- **List Rows**: Minimalist list layouts divided by 1px `#262D3D` horizontal rules. Hover states shift the background to `#161A23` and trigger an indent transition on the typography. Numbering indices (e.g. `01`, `02`) utilize `#FF3B30`, followed by display titles in `#F4F4F0` and stack telemetry in `#5C7C99`.

### Content Modules

- **Project & Case Study Containers**: Non-floating panels. Structured using subtle backgrounds (`#10131A`), 1px borders (`#262D3D`), and 6px radii. Cards favor horizontal splits between project imagery and technical architecture summaries, foregoing generic SaaS metric widgets.
